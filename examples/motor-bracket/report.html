The bracket carries the 500 N shock load with a safety factor of about 2.0 on yield and 0.81 mm deflection. With a realistic motor rotary inertia, mode 2 sits at 131 Hz, 9.5 % above twice running speed (120 Hz), inside the 20 % resonance band. Stiffening or a bump test is recommended before release.

1. Scope

Linear static check under a 500 N shock load plus gravity, and modal check against 3600 rpm.

5. Assumptions

  • Motor rotary inertia 1 t*mm^2 (80 mm diameter motor)
  • Bolt bores fully fixed

6. Limitations

  • Stresses at the bolt holes are not meaningful with fixed bores
  • Shock treated as static; pulses shorter than ~11 ms may amplify up to 2x

7. Conclusions

  • Static strength adequate (SF 2.0)
  • Mode 2 too close to 2x running speed

8. Recommendations

  • Add a corner gusset or thicken the upright, then re-check mode 2
  • Bump-test the prototype
